Editorial note / Skin Atlas
Before you book

Three questions to carry with you.

Save this checklist to keep discovery useful and grounded.

Goal

What is the treatment actually for?

Separate the goal from the marketing name and ask how the session is described.

Practitioner

Who is performing it?

Check practitioner credentials, setting, hygiene practices and whether consultation is offered.

Aftercare

What should you expect after?

Ask about downtime, aftercare and when you should seek professional advice.
